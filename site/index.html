How it works

About five minutes, then a plan that's yours

  1. Tell us about your situation. County, type of home, household, health and diet needs, budget, and storage space.
  2. See what matters most where you live. Plain-language priority bands for each hazard, with the reasons and sources.
  3. Build it like a staircase. Checklists for 7, 14, 30, and 90 days with quantities for your household. Shared items show up once. Check things off as you go.

What this site is, and isn't

  • Is: a free planning tool that turns official guidance into a household checklist.
  • Isn't: an alert system. Utah has no public earthquake early warning, and we never imply one. UGS (opens source in a new tab)
  • Isn't: medical, engineering, legal, or financial advice. Talk to your clinician, a licensed engineer, or your insurer about your situation.
  • Private by design: your answers never leave your browser. Read more.
